================================================================================
  EDITING GUIDE — Content Locations in Paper Portfolio
================================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE:
Every entry below corresponds to a // EDIT: comment in the code. To jump to any
content location, search your editor/IDE for "EDIT: [LABEL]" (e.g., search for
"EDIT: HERO HEADING" to find the hero section title). This convention keeps all
editable content easy to locate without reading through component logic.
